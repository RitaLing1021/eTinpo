OUR CARE INSTRUCTIONS

To maintain your gear and extend its lifespan, regularly clean, dry, and reactivate the DWR (Durable Water Repellent) treatment. This keeps the material's waterproof and breathable properties intact.

Always check the washing instructions for your eTINPO gear. Use specialized detergents to avoid residue that can affect the DWR treatment, and dry your gear at low temperatures to reactivate its coating.

Proper care ensures your gear continues to offer excellent protection and comfort, keeping you ready for any outdoor activity.

  • Do not dry clean
  • Tumble dry at low heat
    (Below 40°C)
  • Do not bleach
  • Do not iron
  • Machine washable, gentle cycle
    (Water temperature below 30°C)
  • Do not use fabric softener
  • Hand Wash
    (Water temperature below 30°C)
  • Rinse twice with clean water
Pre-wash

Before washing, zip up all the zippers, including pockets and underarms, and secure all hook and loop fasteners. For heavily soiled areas, pre-treat with high-performance cleaners such as Nikwax Tech Wash or a mild detergent for targeted cleaning, helping protect the garment and ensure effective cleanliness.

Laundry instructions
  1. It is recommended to wash with plain water or use a mild detergent for cleaning.
    Avoid fabric softener, bleach, soap, laundry powder, and similar detergents.
  2. Wash only one garment at a time, and avoid mixing with other soiled clothes.
  3. Hand washing with clean water or using a specific detergent such as Nikwax is recommended for spot cleaning; follow with 2 rinses in clean water.
  4. If using a washing machine, please use a gentle cycle with clean water
    (below 30°C). Always use a laundry bag.
  5. Please rinse twice with clean water when detergent is applied.
After washing

Please line dry in shade or tumble dry it at a low heat: below 40°C.

Note

Please use a steamer to steam the garment below 100°C. Please do not use an iron.
Please note that fabric softener, dry clean and bleach are prohibited.

Storage Instructions

For long-term storage, hang the garment or lay it flat. Avoid storing with desiccants.
